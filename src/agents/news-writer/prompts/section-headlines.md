# Headlines Section

Generate the top 5 most important stories this reader should know about.

Prioritize based on:
1. Relevance to reader's interests and role
2. Impact/significance of the news
3. Actionability (can they do something with this info?)

For each headline, provide:
- A punchy headline (max 8 words)
- 2-sentence summary
- One line on why it matters to THIS reader specifically

Output format:
**[Headline]**
[Summary]. [source.com]
→ Why it matters: [personalized insight]
